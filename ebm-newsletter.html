01
The Big Idea

Better decisions
begin with better evidence.

Management decisions influence people, performance, culture and organizational success. Yet decisions can easily become influenced by assumptions, habits or personal preferences.

Evidence-Based Management changes the question from “What do I think?” to “What does the available evidence tell us?”


The revised EBM+ approach brings together multiple forms of evidence and connects them with professional expertise, organizational context and stakeholder perspectives.

02
The Four Evidence Pillars

Four perspectives.
One better decision.

01

Scientific Evidence

Research studies, academic literature, systematic reviews and established theories.

02

Organizational Evidence

Internal data, performance indicators, employee metrics and business results.

03

Practitioner Expertise

Professional experience and contextual knowledge from managers and practitioners.

04

Stakeholder Voice

Perspectives from employees, customers, managers and other affected groups.

04
From Decision to Learning

EBM is a cycle,
not a one-time decision.

01. Define Understand the real management problem.
02. Search Gather relevant and trustworthy evidence.
03. Evaluate Assess reliability, relevance and limitations.
04. Decide Select the most evidence-informed option.
05. Measure Track outcomes using meaningful indicators.
06. Improve Learn from results and adapt the decision.
05
The Leadership Imperative

Better evidence.
Healthier people.
Stronger organizations.

Evidence-based management does not replace leadership judgment. It strengthens it.


The future of management is not simply about collecting more data. It is about knowing how to interpret evidence, understand people, recognize uncertainty, make transparent decisions and continuously learn from outcomes.
